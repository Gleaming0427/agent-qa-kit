# EVAL-17 — Gift wrap

- AC-1: the cart shows a "Gift wrap" checkbox; once it is checked, the order summary shows a "Gift wrap" line at €1.00 per unit in the cart, and the total includes it. Gift wrap does not count toward free shipping: shipping is still decided on the subtotal of the products.
- AC-2: unchecking "Gift wrap" removes the line and takes its amount off the total.
